### Description (4,000)

MRPscan is a pricing and billing tool for jewellery showrooms. Photograph the
price tag on any piece and MRPscan reads it, works out the selling price from
live gold rates and your shop's own charges, and turns it into a GST invoice
for your customer, while they are still at the counter.

SCAN A TAG, GET THE MRP
• Point the camera at a tag, or pick a photo of one, and tap Calculate.
• MRPscan reads gross weight, net weight, karat, serial or style number,
  diamonds and colour stones, even on small, tilted or handwritten tags.
• The result shows the full working: pure gold weight, gold amount, diamond
  and colour-stone amounts, labour, wastage and the final MRP.
• Every figure on the card can be checked and corrected before you bill.

LIVE GOLD RATES FROM YOUR BULLION HOUSE
• Follow the bullion house you trade with: MCX, cash and RTGS rates update live
  on the home screen.
• Set your retail and RTGS rates as the bullion rate plus your own change, and
  choose which rate each piece is priced on.

YOUR SHOP'S OWN PRICING RULES
• Masters for gold karats, diamond and colour-stone rates, labour charges,
  labour codes, wastage codes and item codes.
• When a tag carries an item code or a labour or wastage code, MRPscan applies
  your rate for it automatically.

GST INVOICES IN ONE TAP
• Generate a GST invoice for a single piece, or add several pieces to the
  wishlist and bill them together on one invoice.
• Share the invoice PDF on WhatsApp or email, save it, or print it.

FOR THE WHOLE TEAM
• Add your staff with their own login and MPIN, and choose what each person
  can see and change.
• History keeps every scanned tag, wishlist and invoice.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Ask AI, the voice assistant in the bottom bar, answers questions about the
  app in Hindi, English or Hinglish and walks you through each step.
• FAQs in English and Hindi.

SECURE SIGN-IN
• Sign in with your phone number, a one-time code and your 4-digit MPIN.
• Delete your account from Settings at any time.

MRPscan is for GST-registered jewellery businesses and needs an MRPscan
business account.
